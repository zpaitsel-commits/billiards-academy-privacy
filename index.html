Privacy Policy — Billiards Academy

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Developer: Zachary Paitsel
Contact: zpaitsel@gmail.com

Billiards Academy ("we," "us," "our") provides pool drill instruction, AI-based video feedback, and related content through our mobile app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. This policy is not legal advice; it reflects how the app is actually built, as described below.

Information we collect

  • Account information you enter in the app: your name and email address, and your membership tier. This is stored on your device (local app storage) to identify your account in the app and give you access to paid content. We do not operate a separate account server that stores your profile.
  • Practice videos: when you use the AI Feedback feature, you choose a short video of yourself practicing a billiards drill and upload it to our backend for automated analysis (body position and cue-stroke tracking, using MediaPipe pose tracking and heuristic cue detection). The upload is limited to MP4, MOV, or M4V files up to 200 MB. A user identifier is sent with the upload only to associate the video with your analysis job.
  • Practice progress stored on your device: which drills you have completed and how many AI feedback credits you have used this month. This is stored locally on your device to track your progress and enforce your plan's credit limits.
  • Payment information: subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. Google and RevenueCat handle payment data under their own privacy policies. The app syncs your subscription entitlements from RevenueCat on launch so a reinstall does not show a paying subscriber as Free.

We do not use a third-party analytics SDK in the app, and we do not collect your precise location, contacts, or device advertising identifier.

How we use this information

  • To provide the drill library, AI feedback, blog, and membership features.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To show you your own practice progress and remaining AI feedback credits.
  • To communicate with you about your account (for example, a billing issue), if you contact us.

We do not use your practice videos to train AI models.

Third parties we share data with

  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription and one-time purchase payments.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status and entitlements.
  • Render — hosts our AI video-analysis backend, which receives the practice videos you choose to upload and returns the analysis results.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data retention

  • Practice videos: deleted from the backend immediately after analysis completes (including if analysis fails). The analysis result is held only in the backend's in-memory job store so the app can retrieve it, and is not stored in a database.
  • Account and progress data: stored on your device while you use the app. Deleting the app removes the locally stored profile and progress. If you ask us to delete data we hold (for example, correspondence with us), we will do so unless we must keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting zpaitsel@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under the GDPR; if you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in the app or by updating the effective date above. If we ever change how practice videos are handled — for example, retaining them for model improvement — we will disclose that here explicitly before doing so.
